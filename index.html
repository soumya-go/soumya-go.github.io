About Me

Hi, I am an exoplanet scientist based on Earth, fascinated by one of humanity's greatest questions: Are we alone in the Universe? To explore this question, I study exoplanet atmospheres, radiative transfer, planetary magnetic fields, atmospheric chemistry, and planetary evolution. My research combines analytical and numerical modeling to understand the physics and chemistry of Hot Jupiters and other giant exoplanets. Although thousands of exoplanets have already been discovered, I believe we are only beginning to uncover the diversity of planetary systems and the possibility of life beyond Earth. While not exploring other worlds, I enjoy reading books, practicing yoga and meditation, training in karate, and playing the harmonica in the planet Earth. So, Until we find another inhabited world, Earth remains both my home and my observatory.

Featured Research

Featured work on Atmospheric Heat Redistribution Effects on Hot-Jupiter Emission Spectra

This work investigates how atmospheric heat redistribution affects the thermal emission spectra of highly irradiated Hot Jupiters. The spectra reveal the influence of atmospheric circulation and molecular absorption features including H₂O, CO, CO₂ and CH₄.
