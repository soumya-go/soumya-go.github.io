Research

Research Overview

My research focuses on exoplanetary atmospheres, radiative transfer, planetary chemistry, atmospheric retrieval and planetary evolution. I combine analytical and numerical techniques to investigate the physics and chemistry of Hot Jupiters and other giant exoplanets.
